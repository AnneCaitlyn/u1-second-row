# Procedure

## Part 1 - Disassembling the Top Cover

 (1) Disconnect the top cover cable and remove the magnetic front door.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/zh-step-1-1.jpg]

 (2) Remove the top cover from the printer.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/zh-step-1-2.png]

 (3) Turn the top cover over and unlock the clips in the direction shown in the figure. Once unlocked, the Lower Housing can be removed and replaced.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-1-3.1.jpg]

（4） Route the top cover cable into the rear groove. Align the upper housing with the illustrated holes on the lower housing and press to secure in place.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-1-4-2.jpg]

## Part 2 - Removing the Upper Housing

 (1) Turn the Upper Housing upside down and use an H2.0 Hex Key to remove the 2 screws securing the adapter board.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-2-1.png]

 Screw Specification

 Quantity

 ST3 × 8

 2

 (2) Use an H2.0 Hex Key to remove the 6 screws securing the Internal Circulation Kit and External Circulation Kit.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-2-2.png]

 Screw Specification

 Quantity

 ST3 × 8

 6

 (3) Remove the Internal Circulation Kit and External Circulation Kit.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-2-3.jpg]

## Part 3 - Installing the Upper Housing

 (1) Install the Internal Circulation Kit and External Circulation Kit onto the new Upper Housing, and use an H2.0 Hex Key to tighten the 6 securing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-2-2.png]

 Screw Specification

 Quantity

 ST3 × 8

 6

When installing the External Circulation Kit, make sure that the cable does not obstruct the air outlet.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-3-1.png]

 (2) Organize the adapter board cable and fit it into the cable slot, then align the locating pins on the Upper Housing with the notches on the adapter board.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-3-2.jpg]

(3) Use an H2.0 Hex Key to tighten the adapter board securing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/top-zh-2-1.png]

 Screw Specification

 Quantity

 ST3 × 8
