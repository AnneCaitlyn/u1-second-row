# Procedure

## Part1 - Remove the filament and feeder

 (1) Follow the steps below to unload the filament from all toolheads.

[img /u1/u1_all-toolhead-unloading_en.png ]

 (2) Rotate the spool to rewind the filament, then remove the spool (including the holder). Insert the filament end into the hole to prevent tangling.

[img /u1/u1_remove_spool&holder.jpg ]

 If the spool holder is difficult to remove, please refer to: U1 How To Remove The Filament Holder 。

 Before proceeding, please make sure to power off the machine and disconnect power supply.

 (3) Refer to: U1 Video Guide: Machine Unboxing & Assembly 6:15—6:50 to disconnect the 4 mm tubes at the back of the machine, then remove the left and right feeders.

[img /u1/u1_remove_feeders.jpg ]

## Part2 - Remove the machine enclosure

## Part3 - Replace the HUB board

 (1) Disconnect the cables from the HUB board, then remove the old HUB board.

[img /u1/u1_hub-board-wiring_en.jpg ]

 (2) Install the new HUB board and restore the wiring (Insert the docking sensor cables from left to right in order; do not cross the cables).

 (3) Secure the 2 outer screws of group 4 in their respective positions (the middle screw is shared with the rear panel, so do not install it for now).
