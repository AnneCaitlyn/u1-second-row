# Overview

## Location

 The toolheads are mounted on the toolhead docking bracket assembly, arranged from left to right as Toolhead 1, 2, 3, and 4.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_position.jpg]

## Terminology

 Different from our official term toolhead, some people may use the following terms to describe this component:

 (1) Toolhead assembly

 (2) Tool head

 (3) Print head

# Procedure

## Step 1. Unload filament

 (1) Tap the filament info section on the home page.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_en-unload-1.jpg]

 (2) Tap "Unload mode".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_en-unload-2.jpg]

 (3) Choose the target toolhead (e.g., Toolhead 1) and tap "Unload".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_en-unload-3.jpg]

 (4) Wait for the printer to finish unloading, then tap "OK".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_en-unload-4.jpg]

 (5) Return to the home page, tap the upper-left corner to check the nozzle temperature, and wait until it cools below 40 °C.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_en-unload-5.jpg]

## Step 2. Replace the toolhead

 Before continuing, please power off the machine and unplug it！

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_power_off.jpg]

 (1) Press the quick connector to remove the filament tube.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_filament-tube-pull-out.jpg]

 (2) Use an H2.0 hex key to remove the 2 screws and unplug the toolhead cable.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_usb-cable-pull-out.jpg]

 (3) Insert the H2.0 hex key into the middle holes between the 2 locating pins until you hear a click sound , then remove the toolhead.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_manually-detach-3.jpg]

 (4) Use an H2.0 hex key to adjust the toolhead latch until two red markings are visible:

 Left ：Toolhead 1 – latch to the left limit

 Right ：Toolhead 2/3/4 – latch to the right limit

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_latch-position.jpg]

 (5) Align the latch hole with the locating pin on the swapper and push the toolhead inward until it stops.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead-all-back-locating-pins_position.jpg]

 (6) Keep pushing inward and move the toolhead slightly so the 2 side locating pins fit into the bracket holes.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead-all-side-locating-pins_position.jpg]

 (7) Reconnect the toolhead cable and tighten the 2 screws with an H2.0 hex key.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_reconnect-usb-cable.jpg]

 (8) Insert the filament tube.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead1_insert-filament-tube.jpg]
