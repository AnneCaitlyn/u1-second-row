# Overview

## Location

 The nozzle cleaning brush and the nozzle parking pad are installed on the nozzle wiper, located right below the toolhead nozzle. Their functions are as follows:

 Nozzle cleaning brush: Wipes the nozzle to keep it clean

 Nozzle parking pad: Prevents filament leakage from the toolhead nozzle that is not in use

## Part 1. Installing the Nozzle Cleaning Brush

### Step 1. Power off the U1 and unplug the power cable

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_power_off_the_machine.png]

### Step 2. Remove the toolhead

 This example shows how to replace the nozzle cleaning brush and nozzle parking pad for Toolhead 4.

### Step 3. Remove the old nozzle cleaning brush

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_cleaning_brushes_&_parking_pads_kit_replacement_guide_remove_old_brush.jpeg]

### Step 4. Install the new nozzle cleaning brush

 The new brush comes with an adhesive backing. Clean the surface of the holder first, then attach the new brush securely.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_cleaning_brushes_&_parking_pads_kit_replacement_guide_brush_back.png]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_cleaning_brushes_&_parking_pads_kit_replacement_guide_location2.jpeg]

## Part 2. Installing the Nozzle Parking Pad

### Step 1. Remove the old nozzle parking pad

 The parking pad is clipped onto the nozzle wiper holder. Simply pull it outward to remove it.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_cleaning_brushes_&_parking_pads_kit_replacement_guide_pad_position.jpeg]

### Step 2. Install the new nozzle parking pad

 Insert the new parking pad into the holder and make sure it is firmly fixed in place.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_cleaning_brushes_&_parking_pads_kit_replacement_guide_location2.jpeg]
