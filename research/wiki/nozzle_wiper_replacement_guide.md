# Overview

## Location

 The nozzle wiper is located directly below the toolhead nozzle.

 As shown in the picture, the left nozzle wiper is for toolhead 1 and should be installed on the far left. The right nozzle wiper is for toolheads 2, 3, and 4.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_wiper_position.png]

## Terminology

 Different from our official term, some people may use the following terms to describe this component:

# Procedure

## Step 1. Move the toolhead and print platform

 Select the toolhead (e.g., No.1) corresponding to the nozzle wiper to be replaced, then tap "Detach Toolhead" on the touchscreen.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_detach_toolhead.png]

 Move the print platform to the lower limit position.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_print_platform_move_down1.png]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_print_platform_move_down2.jpeg]

 Turn off the machine and unplug the power cable, then manually move the toolhead away from the nozzle wiper.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_power_off_the_machine.png]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_move_away.png]

## Step 2. Remove the old nozzle wiper

 Use the H2.0 hex key to unscrew the 2 fixing screws, then remove the old nozzle wiper.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_wiper_screw.png]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_nozzle_wiper.jpeg]

## Step 3. Install the new nozzle wiper

 Install the new nozzle wiper and use the H2.0 hex key to tighten the 2 fixing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_wiper_screw.png]
