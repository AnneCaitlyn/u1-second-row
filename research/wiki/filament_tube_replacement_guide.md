# Overview

## Location

 The filament tube between the toolhead and the top panel has a specification of outer diameter 6 mm, inner diameter 4.6 mm, length 650 mm.

 Both ends are secured with quick-release fittings, which must be pressed to remove the tube.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_filament_tube_replacement_guide_location.png]

# Procedure

## Step 1. Unload filament

 Tap the specified area on the touchscreen (Highlighted in red in the image below).

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament1.png]

 Tap "Unload Mode".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament2.png]

 Select the target toolhead (e.g., No.2) and tap "Unload".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament5.jpg]

 Wait for the system to automatically complete the filament unloading.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament6.jpg]

 Turn the spool to retract the filament.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_retract_filament.png]

## Step 2. Remove the old filament tube

 Press the quick connectors at positions 1 and 2, then remove both ends of the filament tube.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_filament_tube1.png]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_filament_tube2.png]

 Remove the filament tube from the filament tube holder.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_filament_tube3.png]

 Complete.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_filament_tube4.jpeg]

## Step 3. Install the new filament tube

 Insert both ends of the new filament tube into the quick connectors, then press the filament tube into the filament tube holder.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_install_filament_tube.png]

 Finally, use the clips to secure the filament tube and the toolhead USB cable.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_clip_position.jpg]
