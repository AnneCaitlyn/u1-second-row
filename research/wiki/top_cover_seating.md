# 1. What You May Notice

 When installing the U1 Top Cover, you may notice that the four mounting posts underneath the cover do not fully seat in the silicone pads on top of the printer. Pressing down on the cover will seat the posts, but once the pressure is released, the foam may cause the cover to lift slightly, causing the posts to partially come out of the silicone pads.

 This is within the normal range of assembly tolerances and does not affect the installation, sealing performance, or normal use of the Top Cover. 

 Updated September 30, 2026: Previously, we described the seating condition as "within the normal range of assembly tolerances." This description was not accurate, and we sincerely apologize for any confusion it may have caused. The seating condition does not affect the Top Cover's core functionality, sealing performance, or safe use. Please refer to the information below for more details and available improvements.

# 2. Why This Happens

## Why can't the mounting posts fully seat in the silicone pads?

 The four mounting posts at the corners of the Top Cover fit into the silicone pads on top of the printer. They provide additional positioning for the Top Cover.

 During mass production, we increased the thickness of the sealing foam on the bottom of the Top Cover from 3 mm to 5 mm to improve sealing performance.

 However, the increased foam thickness reduces the depth to which the four mounting posts can engage with the silicone pads, so the posts may not remain fully seated.

## Why doesn't this affect the Top Cover's positioning or sealing?

 The overall support, positioning, and sealing of the Top Cover do not rely solely on the fit between the four mounting posts and the silicone pads:

 The surrounding edges provide support, positioning and primary movement restraint , limiting the movement of the Top Cover and keeping it stable.

 The sealing foam on the bottom provides the seal by maintaining sufficient contact with the top of the printer.

 Therefore, the Top Cover will remain properly positioned and sealed even if the mounting posts are not fully seated, as long as the surrounding edges are aligned and the Top Cover is correctly installed.

 We understand that a closer fit at the mounting posts can help reduce movement when external force is applied. We are currently preparing official connectors to further improve your experience with the Top Cover.

# 3. How to Improve the Fit

## Official Connectors

 We are preparing the tooling and production of the connectors to improve the fit around the four mounting posts in certain cases. Once tooling, testing, and validation are complete, we will provide them free of charge, with shipping costs and any applicable taxes covered by Snapmaker.

 The exact availability date will depend on the time needed to complete production and arrange delivery. We are actively working to move the process forward and will share further updates through our official channels.

 If you are currently experiencing the seating situation described above, you can fill out this form to register your information. We will contact you based on the information provided once the connectors are ready.

 Whether you register in advance will not affect your eligibility to receive the official connectors later. We will notify all users who have purchased the U1 Top Cover through our official channels and provide details on how to receive the connectors. You can then decide whether to request them based on your actual needs.

 In the meantime, if you would like to improve the fit around the four mounting posts, you can print and install the mounting post connectors provided by us. These connectors have undergone internal testing and validation and can be used as an interim measure. Once the official connectors are ready, we will arrange shipment to you as soon as possible.

## Installation

 Install one connector onto each of the four mounting posts of the Top Cover.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/fnr.png]

 The front and rear mounting posts use different connectors. Make sure to install them in the correct positions:

 1-bump connectors go on the  front  mounting posts.

 2-bump connectors go on the  rear  mounting posts.

 If you have any further questions or encounter other issues, please submit a support ticket through https://snapmaker.formcrafts.com/u1-troubleshooting-request . Our dedicated support team will be more than willing to assist you.

# 4. Future Improvements

 We are aware of feedback from some users regarding the fit between the mounting posts and the silicone pads.

In parallel, we have started a project to revise the Top Cover mold. One improvement currently under consideration is to  slightly increase the length of the mounting posts  to improve their engagement with the silicone pads while maintaining the sealing performance of the Top Cover. 

 Updated September 30, 2026: Based on user feedback and further evaluation, we are preparing the tooling and production of official connectors to further improve the fit around the mounting posts. Once testing and validation are complete, we will provide them free of charge to users who need them. These connectors will also be included with Top Covers produced in the future, with the rollout phased in over time. For details on how to request the connectors, please refer to How to Improve the Fit above.
