# Overview

## Location

 The toolhead docking bracket is located on the rear beam of the U1.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_docking_bracket_location.jpg]

## Difficulty and Time Estimate

 Difficulty ： ★★ ☆☆☆ (Easy)

 Estimated Time ：20 minutes

## Where to Buy

 This accessory is expected to be available on the official Snapmaker online store soon. Please stay tuned.

## Tools and Parts Required

 H2.0 hex key

 New toolhead docking bracket

# Procedure

## Step 1. Detach the toolhead from the docking bracket

 Before proceeding, please turn off the machine and unplug the power cable!

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_power_off_the_machine.png]

 Detach the 4 toolheads from the docking bracket, and place them on the print platform.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_place_toolhead_on_print_bed.jpg]

 For head No.1: Slide to the right until the locating pin is fully visible.

 For heads No.2/3/4: Slide the toolhead to the left until the locating pin is fully visible.

## Step 2. Remove the nozzle wiper

 Use the H2.0 hex key to unscrew the 8 fixing screws, then remove the 4 nozzle wipers.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_wiper_screw2.jpg]

## Step 3. Remove the old toolhead docking bracket

 Use the H2.0 hex key to unscrew the 4 fixing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_docking_bracket_screw2.jpg]

 Carefully move the bracket outward and flip it over. Press the clip indicated by the arrow while pulling out the 4 cables of the docking Hall sensor board.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_disconect_docking_hall_sensor_board3.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_disconect_docking_hall_sensor_board2.jpg]

 Remove the old toolhead docking bracket.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_docking_bracket_remove2.jpg]

## Step 3. Install the new toolhead docking bracket

 Connect the 4 cables of the docking Hall sensor board, install the new toolhead docking bracket, and use the H2.0 hex key to tighten the 4 fixing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_docking_bracket_screw2.jpg]

## Step 4. Install the nozzle wiper

 Install the 4 nozzle wipers and use the H2.0 hex key to tighten the 8 fixing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_wiper_screw2.jpg]

 The left nozzle wiper is for toolhead 1 and should be installed on the far left, and the 3 right nozzle wipers should be installed on the right side.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_nozzle_wiper_position.png]

## Step 5. Place the toolhead onto the docking bracket

 Before installing the toolhead, please make sure the sliders are in the correct positions. The slider for Toolhead No. 1 should be set to the far left, while the sliders for Toolheads No. 2, 3, and 4 should all be set to the far right.

 You can use a hex key to adjust the slider and make sure the red mark on the slider is visible.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_replacement_guide_step5.jpg]

 Align the hole on the toolhead with the long locating pin on the docking bracket, and push inward until it stops.

 The illustrated side of the toolhead should face inward.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/安装打印头1.png]

 Align the two locating pins on the toolhead with the holes shown on the mount.

 For No.1 head: hole is to the left of the pins

 For No.2/3/4 heads: hole is to the right of the pins

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/安装打印头2.png]
