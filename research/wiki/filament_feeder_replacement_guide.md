# Overview

## Location

 The filament feeders are located at the rear of both sides of the machine. Filament from the spools is delivered to the toolheads via the filament feeder, so filament unloading must be completed before replacement.

## Terminology

 Different from our official term, some people may use the following terms to describe this component:

 Autofeeder

 Filament advancer

 Feeder unit

 Power feeder

# Procedure

## Step 1. Unload filament

 Tap the specified area on the touchscreen (Highlighted in red in the image below).

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament1.png]

 Tap "Unload Mode".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament2.png]

 Select the toolheads.

For replacing the left filament feeder: Select Toolheads 1 and 2, then tap "Unload".

For replacing the right filament feeder: Select Toolheads 3 and 4, then tap "Unload".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament3.png]

 Wait for the system to automatically complete the filament unloading.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament4.png]

 Turn the spool to retract the filament.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_main-control-board_unload_filament4.png]

## Step 2. Remove the old filament feeder

 Press the clip at the marked location.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_hub_board_step5_remove_the_filament_feeder_on_both_sides1.png]

 While pressing the clip, slide the filament feeder in the indicated direction to remove it.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_filament_feeder2.png]

 Disconnect the filament feeder cable.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_filament_feeder3.png]

 Press the quick connectors to remove the filament tubes.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_filament_feeder4.png]

## Step 3. Install the new filament feeder

 Reconnect the filament feeder cable and organize it as shown.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/ 
u1_main-control-board_reinstall_filament_feeder2.png]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/ 
u1_main-control-board_reinstall_filament_feeder3.png]

 Align the feeder clips with the corresponding positions, then push the feeder into place in the direction shown to complete installment.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/ 
u1_main-control-board_reinstall_filament_feeder4.png]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/
