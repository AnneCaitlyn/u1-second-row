# Overview

## Location

 The toolhead male mount is the front cover of toolhead. It is divided into two types: left and right. The left male mount is used for toolhead 1, and the right male mount is used for toolhead 2/3/4.

 Left male mount:

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_male_mount_left.jpg]

 Right male mount:

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_male_mount_right.jpg]

 Difficulty ： ★★ ☆☆☆ (Easy)

# Procedure

## Step 1. Remove the toolhead

### 1.1 Unload filament

 Tap the specified area on the touchscreen (Highlighted in red in the image below).

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament1.png]

 Tap "Unload Mode".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament2.png]

 Select the target toolhead (e.g., No.2) and tap "Unload".

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament5.jpg]

 Wait for the system to automatically complete the filament unloading.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_unload_filament6.jpg]

### 1.2 Detach the toolhead from the docking bracket

 Before proceeding, please turn off the machine and unplug the power cable!

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_power_off_the_machine.png]

 For head No.1: Slide to the right until the locating pin is fully visible.

 For heads No.2/3/4: Slide the toolhead to the left until the locating pin is fully visible.

### 1.3 Remove filament and guide tube

 Remove the three cable clips.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_clip_position.jpg]

 Press the quick-release fitting.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_press-quick-connector.png]

 Remove the filament and the filament tube.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_pull-out-filament-with-its-tube.png]

### 1.4 Disconnect USB and remove the toolhead

 Use the H2.0 hex key to remove the two mounting screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_usb-cable_screws.png]

 Unplug the USB cable and then carefully remove the toolhead.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_usb-cable_pull-out.png]

## Step 2. Remove the old toolhead male mount

### 2.1 Remove top cover and back cover

 Use the H2.0 hex key to remove the three screws securing the top cover of the toolhead, then remove the top cover.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/移除打印头上盖板.png]

 Remove the back cover of the toolhead.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/取下打印头后盖板.jpg]

### 2.2 Unscrew screws of toolhead PCB

 Use H1.5 and H2.0 hex keys respectively to unscrew screws 1 and 2 of toolhead PCB.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_pcb_screw2.jpg]

 Lift the toolhead PCB carefully, ensure that the hole in the middle is positioned above the filament runout sensor.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_lift_toolhead_pcb.jpg]

### 2.3 Unscrew screws of extruder unit

 Use the H1.5 hex key to unscrew fixing screw 1 and screw 2, and use the H2.0 hex key to unscrew fixing screw 3 and screw 4.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_extruder_unit_screw2.jpg]

### 2.4 Unscrew screws of hotend

 Use the H2.0 hex key to unscrew the 2 fixing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_hotend_screws.jpg]

### 2.5 Unscrew screws of hotend cooling assembly

 Move the hotend aside, use the H1.5 hex key to unscrew the 3 fixing screws.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_hotend-cooling-fan_base-screws.png]

 Remove the old toolhead male mount.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_remove_toolhead_male_mount.jpg]

## Step 3. Install the new toolhead male mount

### 3.1 Tighten the screws on the toolhead male mount

 Install the new toolhead male mount, then use the H1.5 hex key to tighten the 3 fixing screws of hotend cooling assembly.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_hotend-cooling-fan_base-screws.png]

 Use the H2.0 hex key to tighten the 2 fixing screws of hotend.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_hotend_screws.jpg]

 Use the H1.5 hex key to tighten fixing screw 1 and screw 2, and use the H2.0 hex key to tighten fixing screw 3 and screw 4.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_extruder_unit_screw2.jpg]

 Use the H1.5 and H2.0 hex keys to tighten PCB fixing screw 1 and screw 2 respectively.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_pcb_screw2.jpg]

### 3.2 Install top cover and back cover

 Install the top cover of toolhead.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/移除打印头上盖板.png]

 Install the back cover of toolhead.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/取下打印头.jpg]

## Step 4. Install the toolhead

### 4.1 Place the toolhead onto the docking bracket

 Before installing the toolhead, please make sure the sliders are in the correct positions. The slider for Toolhead No. 1 should be set to the far left, while the sliders for Toolheads No. 2, 3, and 4 should all be set to the far right.

 You can use a hex key to adjust the slider and make sure the red mark on the slider is visible.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead_replacement_guide_step5.jpg]

 Align the hole on the toolhead with the long locating pin on the docking bracket, and push inward until it stops.

 The illustrated side of the toolhead should face inward.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/安装打印头1.png]

 Align the two locating pins on the toolhead with the holes shown on the mount.

 For No.1 head: hole is to the left of the pins

 For No.2/3/4 heads: hole is to the right of the pins

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/安装打印头2.png]

### 4.2 Connect and secure the USB cable

 Plug in the USB cable and fasten the two screws with the H2.0 hex key.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_usb-cable_screws.png]

### 4.3 Load filament

 While pressing part 1, as highlighted in the image below, insert the filament into part 2 until it reaches the end.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/进料.png]

 Finally, connect the filament tube. The toolhead installation is now complete.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1_assets/new/取耗材和导料管.png]
