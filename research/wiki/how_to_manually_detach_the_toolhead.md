# Compatibility

 This article applies to the following model and module. Please take note when referencing:

 Model: U1

# Applicable scenarios

 When you need to detach the toolhead from the swapper while the printer is powered off.

# Procedure

## Toolhead-1

 Facing the back of the printer.Align the toolhead hole with the positioning pin and keep it against the right inner wall.Push the toolhead and swapper inward to the stop, then left until the pin fits into the hole on the backing bracket and the toolhead detaches.

 Facing the back of the printer.Align the toolhead hole with the positioning pin and keep it against the left inner wall.Push the toolhead and swapper inward to the stop, then right until the pin fits into the hole on the backing bracket and the toolhead detaches.
