# U1 X carbon rod assembly replacement guide | Snapmaker Wiki

Source: https://wiki.snapmaker.com/en/snapmaker_u1/troubleshooting/u1_carbon_rod_replacement_guide

# Overview

## Location

 The X carbon rod assembly is located on the X-axis and is connected to the toolhead converter. Before replacing it, you need to remove the toolhead converter.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_position.jpg]

## Difficulty and Time Estimate

 Difficulty ： ★★★★★ (difficult)

 Estimated Time ：180mins

## Where to Buy

 This accessory is expected to be available on the official Snapmaker online store soon. Please stay tuned.

## Tools and Parts Required

 H1.5 Hex key

 H2.0 Hex key

 Tweezers / Flathead screwdriver

 Strong needle-nose pliers

 Fine wire or Copper wire

 Zip tie

 New carbon rod

## Step 4. Remove the toolhead swapper

 Use an H2.0 hex key to unscrew 2 screws and remove the part cooling fan air duct and part cooling fan shroud

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_part-cooling-vent-kit_screws.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_part-cooling-vent-kit_remove1.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_part-cooling-vent-kit_remove2.jpg]

 Disconnect the part cooling fan cable then use an H2.0 hex key to unscrew 2 screws and remove the part cooling fan

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_part-cooling-fan_wiring-and-screws.jpg]

 Use an H1.5 hex key to unscrew 4 screws and remove the part cooling fan adapter board

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_toolhead-fan-adapter-boards_screws.jpg]

 Use an H2.0 hex key to unscrew 4 screws and remove the toolhead swapper base

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_female-socket_screws.jpg]

 Use an H2.0 hex key to sequentially unscrew 8 screws and separate X-axis slider-1 and X-axis slider-2, note that X-axis slider-2 has 4 anti-backlash springs, after disassembling the X-axis sliders, place them safely to avoid loss

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_x-axis-slider_screws.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_x-axis-slider1-and-2_with-4-anti-backlash-springs.jpg]

 Use an H2.0 hex key to unscrew 2 screws securing the end tensioning movable block, note these 2 screws have lock nuts on the back of the slider, remove and store them safely

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_end-tensioning-movable-block_screws.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_end-tensioning-movable-block_locknuts.jpg]

 Slowly remove the end tensioning movable block and torsion springs, 2 each.

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_end-tensioning-movable-block_and_torsion-spring_remove.jpg]

 On the outer surface of X-axis slider-2, use an H2.0 hex key to push out the timing belt fixing block and remove the end of the timing belts fixed here

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_belt-fixing-block_position.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_belt-fixing-block-position_remove.jpg]

## Step 5. Replace the carbon rod

 The XY timing belts are divided into upper and lower belts, remove both ends of each belt simultaneously from points 1 and 2

 Note not to completely remove the timing belt from the frame 

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_xy-timing-belts_pull-out-step-1.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_xy-timing-belts_pull-out-step-2.jpg]

 The machine side frames have holes for removing their securing screws

Slightly adjust the carbon rod position, use an H2.0 hex key to unscrew a total of 8 screws

After removing the screws, the metal plates used to fix the carbon rod on both sides will fall off

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod-screws_1-4.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod-screws_5-8.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_remove-step-3.jpg]

 Remove the carbon rod following the illustration

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_remove-step-1.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_remove-step-2.jpg]

 Install the carbon rod following the illustration, note the side of the carbon rod with the arrow should be closer to the front of the machine

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_mark.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_install-step-1.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_install-step-2.jpg]

 Adjust the carbon rod position so it is perpendicular to the Y-axis, and the Y-axis slider on the carbon rod side closest to the front of the machine is aligned with the linear bearing

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_install-step-3.jpg]

 Use fine wire or copper wire to secure the metal plates on both sides of the Y-axis that were originally connected to the carbon rod, pass the wire through the holes on the top of the frame on both sides, through the corresponding holes on the metal plates, then out from the other end and fix it on the frame as shown

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_fixed1.jpg]

 Use an H2.0 hex key to tighten 4 screws on each side of the frame, first tighten the 2 lower screws, then disconnect and remove the wire/copper wire, then tighten the 2 upper screws

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_fixed2.jpg]

[img https://wiki.snapmaker.com/u1/u1_carbon-rod_fixed3.jpg]

## Step 6. Restore the toolhead swapper
